About Us

Clean clothes for your daily OOTD. That one simple idea is how RAN Clothing began, and it still guides everything we make.

  • Where it all started: RAN Clothing began as a small idea among friends in Davao City who were tired of the same problem: good-looking clothes were either too expensive or too uncomfortable to wear every day. We started with a few sketches, a lot of late-night conversations, and one goal: make clothes we would be proud to wear ourselves.
  • Clean, comfortable, everyday style: Our pieces are simple on purpose. Oversized tees with a washed-black look, cozy hoodies and relaxed cargo pants are easy to mix and match, so getting dressed takes less time and always feels right. No loud logos and no trying too hard, just clean basics with attitude.
  • Proudly from Ecoland, Davao City: Our home is Ecoland Drive, and the city's laid-back, hardworking spirit shapes our designs. We make clothes for students, workers and creatives who want to look good from morning classes to late-night hangouts without spending too much.
  • Low prices, real quality: Stylish clothes shouldn't be a luxury. We keep our prices low by staying small, skipping the unnecessary extras and putting our effort into fit, fabric and finishing. When you buy from RAN Clothing, you get pieces that last through many washes and many outfits.
  • Shop anytime, anywhere: Life gets busy, so our store is always open. Browse our latest drops, choose what you like and place your order whenever it suits you, whether that's in the middle of the day or well past midnight.
  • More than just clothes: We want everyone who wears RAN to feel confident and comfortable being themselves. Every order is a small step in growing a local brand that treats its customers like friends, and we're only getting started.

Have a question, a suggestion or a special request? We'd love to hear from you.
